Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

The short version: MileMarker uses your information to plan your RV trips, build your packing lists and remember your trips. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

This policy explains what MileMarker ("we", "us") collects when you use the MileMarker trip planner, how we use it, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you plan without an account, your trips, packing lists and settings stay in your browser's storage on your device. We don't receive them, except as described under "Trip planning" below.

When you create an account, we store:

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign up with email, we store your password only in a scrambled (hashed) form that we can't read. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google.
  • Profile: what you choose to add, such as your home base, your RV's type, length, year and nickname, and the names and ages of the people who usually travel with you.
  • Trips: start and end points, dates, daily routes, stops and campground suggestions, and your edits.
  • Packing lists: the people on the trip (names and ages you enter), food preferences, items, notes and what you've checked off.
  • Drive logs: when you use "Start trip", the times you start each day, take breaks and arrive. MileMarker does not track your GPS location.
  • Chat: messages you send to the MileMarker assistant and its replies (we keep your most recent 200 messages).
  • Basic technical data: our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) for security and troubleshooting.

Food preferences can include allergies or dietary needs. Only enter what you're comfortable sharing; it's used only to build your packing list.

How we use it

  • To plan routes, daily drives, breaks and stays, and to build packing lists.
  • To save your trips and settings so they're on every device you sign in on.
  • To personalize plans, for example using your home base, your usual travelers and your average driving speed and break length from past drive logs.
  • To send account emails: sign-up confirmation codes and password-reset codes. We don't send marketing email.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse (for example, daily chat limits) and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

Trip planning

To work out driving distances, the app sends the locations of your stops to the OpenStreetMap routing service. When AI planning or chat is used, your trip details and messages are sent to Anthropic to generate a response. These requests don't include your password.

How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, trips, packing lists and drive logs: until you delete them or your account.
  • Chat history: your most recent 200 messages.
  • Email codes: they expire after 15 minutes and are deleted once used.
  • Server logs: kept by our hosting provider for a limited time under their own policies.

Your choices

  • Edit: change your profile, trips and packing lists in the app at any time.
  • Delete a trip: tap "Delete trip" on the home page.
  • Delete your account: tap your name, then "Delete my account". This permanently deletes your account, profile, trips, packing lists, drive logs and chat history.
  • Use without an account: you can plan trips without signing in; they stay on your device.
  • Ask us: to request a copy of your information or ask any question, email us (below).

Security

Connections to MileMarker are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed, sign-in sessions expire after 30 days, and secrets such as API keys are kept on the server, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Children

MileMarker is meant for adults planning trips. It isn't directed at children under 13, and children shouldn't create accounts. Parents may add their children's first names and ages to a trip to build a packing list; this stays in the parent's account and can be deleted at any time.

Safety note

Plans, times and distances are estimates. Always check bridge clearances, road restrictions and campground details yourself, and don't use the app while driving — let a passenger tap the buttons.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date at the top and let signed-in users know in the app.
